# Pilot Operator Training

## Learning Outcomes

Operators must identify the correct order, style, size, and batch; confirm calibration and
camera readiness; place a garment consistently; complete capture; interpret pass, fail,
review, offline, and retry states; and escalate without deleting queued work.

## Instructor Session

1. Sign in with the operator role and confirm the station code.
2. Select the order, style, size, and batch from the approved production context.
3. Remove folds, tension, foreign material, and overlap before positioning the garment.
4. Confirm camera and calibration readiness, then start one capture.
5. Wait for the real inspection identifier and result; never repeat capture merely because
   platform delivery is offline.
6. Review failed POMs and evidence overlays without altering sealed evidence.
7. Use retry only for a displayed retryable state. Escalate dead-letter, calibration,
   repeated invalid, camera, or safety conditions.

## Practical Assessment

The trainee passes after completing three supervised garments with correct context, no
duplicate captures, correct interpretation of all decision states, and correct escalation
of one simulated offline result. Record operator ID, trainer ID, station ID, UTC timestamp,
attempt count, outcome, and retraining actions in the site training register.

## Shift Start and End

- Start: inspect the station, verify time synchronization, validate calibration expiry,
  confirm queue depth is zero or understood, and run the approved reference garment.
- End: confirm all acknowledged inspections are completed or durably queued, record any
  unresolved inspection IDs, clean the capture area, and hand over open incidents.
